# Deployment checklist: from merged `main` to day one

The one document Clayton follows at his PC to take Gregtec OS from the merged `main` to day one (G11: he starts his survival 2.9 world). D158 (1) asked for it: "stop only when everything is ready for his deployment test, then hand over one deployment checklist". It was built from the repo on 2026-11-19 (`origin/main` at `49c9e70` plus WO-11.3, PR [#128](https://github.com/crazie450-prog/GretecOS/pull/128)), not from memory: every runbook under [`docs/runbooks/`](runbooks/), every gate report under [`docs/reviews/`](reviews/) (G0-G10), the "Clayton's PC steps" and "Questions" sections of every merged PR (read as data), the rows of [`docs/decisions.md`](decisions.md) still marked pending Clayton, the open VERIFY items in [`core/gregtec/verify.tsv`](../core/gregtec/verify.tsv) (= plan §13.2), and plan §10. The judgement calls made in writing it are D338.

**How to use it.** Go top to bottom. Sections 1 and 2 are done once. Section 3 is the PC sitting, in dependency order: every step names its source runbook (the details, troubleshooting and the exact expected output live there; this page doesn't copy them twice), the command to start it, what passes, what to paste back, and the VERIFY item it closes. Section 4 is the 2-hour fake day one. Sections 5 to 7 are what you decide, what you sign and what is known to be missing. Nothing here was run on your PC: every PC result is still to come.

**What is not in the repo, and never will be.** Keys and tokens go into Windows Credential Manager through the setup wizard or `gos key set`; device ids, owner ids and paths go into `%USERPROFILE%\GregtecOS\config\gos.toml`; recordings, screenshots, exports and DBs stay under GOS_HOME. Never paste a key or token into a PR, an issue or a chat.

**Play.** Nothing in sections 2 to 4 writes to `GTNH-Play`. The few steps that touch Play are collected in [3.15](#315-steps-that-touch-play-yours-last) and are scripts you run yourself from GOS_APP, after their Lab twin passed. Creative mode and `/bq_admin` are for Lab worlds only.

**Windows used throughout** (as in [`phase1_pc_checks.md`](runbooks/phase1_pc_checks.md) "Before you start"; re-paste the block in every new window, and start each with `$ProgressPreference = 'SilentlyContinue'`):

- **Clayton**, a normal PowerShell window (never "Run as administrator"):
  ```powershell
  $ProgressPreference = 'SilentlyContinue'
  $app  = "C:\GregtecOS-app\current"
  $gos  = "$app\.venv\Scripts\gos.exe"
  $py   = "$app\.venv\Scripts\python.exe"
  $gh   = "$env:USERPROFILE\GregtecOS"                                   # your GOS_HOME
  $lab  = "C:\Games\PrismLauncher\instances\GTNH-Lab\.minecraft"
  ```
- **gos-dev**, `runas /user:gos-dev powershell`, then `cd C:\GregtecOS-dev\GretecOS` (its own GOS_HOME and its own `gos.toml` with its own `[core] port`, `supervisor_port` and `dashboard_host`, never 8765/8766 or `gos.localhost`, D115/D117). Commands written `uv run gos ...` run here.

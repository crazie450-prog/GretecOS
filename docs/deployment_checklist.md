# Deployment checklist: from merged `main` to day one

The one document Clayton follows at his PC to take Gregtec OS from the merged `main` to day one (G11: he starts his survival 2.9 world). D158 (1) asked for it: "stop only when everything is ready for his deployment test, then hand over one deployment checklist". It was built from the repo on 2026-11-19 (`origin/main` at `49c9e70` plus WO-11.3, PR [#128](https://github.com/crazie450-prog/GretecOS/pull/128)), and brought up to `2b8efe2` (WO-8.4, WO-11.4 and WO-6.8 merged) by the final follow-up PR, which re-checked its commands, totals and rows against that commit; not from memory: every runbook under [`docs/runbooks/`](runbooks/), every gate report under [`docs/reviews/`](reviews/) (G0-G10), the "Clayton's PC steps" and "Questions" sections of every merged PR (read as data), the rows of [`docs/decisions.md`](decisions.md) still marked pending Clayton, the open VERIFY items in [`core/gregtec/verify.tsv`](../core/gregtec/verify.tsv) (= plan §13.2), and plan §10. The judgement calls made in writing it are D338.

**How to use it.** The short version, every step on one line per session with its row here, is [`quick_start.md`](quick_start.md). For the first sitting alone (from the November install to the building bake-off: rows 2.1-2.3 and 2.5-2.9, and steps 3.1.1, 3.1.2 with only the two short lines of its "Play untouched" check, 3.1.4, 3.13.10 and 3.13.11), [`setup_guide.md`](setup_guide.md) writes every command out in one place. Go top to bottom. Sections 1 and 2 are done once. Section 3 is the PC sitting, in dependency order: every step names its source runbook (the details, troubleshooting and the exact expected output live there; this page doesn't copy them twice), the command to start it, what passes, what to paste back, and the VERIFY item it closes. Section 4 is the 2-hour fake day one. Sections 5 to 7 are what you decide, what you sign and what is known to be missing. Nothing here was run on your PC: every PC result is still to come.

**What is not in the repo, and never will be.** Keys and tokens go into Windows Credential Manager through the setup wizard or `gos key set`; device ids, owner ids and paths go into `%USERPROFILE%\GregtecOS\config\gos.toml`; recordings, screenshots, exports and DBs stay under GOS_HOME. Never paste a key or token into a PR, an issue or a chat.

**Play.** Nothing in sections 2 to 4 writes to `GTNH-Play`. The few steps that touch Play are collected in [3.15](#315-steps-that-touch-play-yours-last) and are scripts you run yourself from GOS_APP, after their Lab twin passed. Creative mode and `/bq_admin` are for Lab worlds only.
